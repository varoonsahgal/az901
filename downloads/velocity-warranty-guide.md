# Velocity Motors Warranty Guide — 2026 edition (excerpt)

## 1. Basic (bumper-to-bumper) coverage
All new Velocity vehicles are covered for 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first, against defects in materials or workmanship. Wear items (brake pads, wiper blades, tires, bulbs) are excluded after 12 months / 12,000 miles.

## 2. Powertrain coverage
Engine, transmission, drive axles and (for hybrids) the hybrid control module are covered for 6 years or 70,000 miles.

## 3. Corrosion
Perforation from rust is covered for 7 years with unlimited miles. Surface rust is not covered.

## 4. Electric vehicle components (Velocity Volt)
4.1 Drive unit and on-board charger: 8 years or 100,000 miles.
4.2 High-voltage battery: 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first, against defects AND against capacity loss below 70% of the original rated capacity.
4.3 Transferability: all Velocity warranties transfer automatically to subsequent owners at no cost for the remainder of the original term.
4.4 Exclusions: damage from flooding, racing or track use, or charging with non-approved chargers is not covered.

## 5. Roadside assistance
24/7 roadside assistance (towing to the nearest Velocity dealer, flat-tire change, jump start, lockout) is included for 5 years with unlimited miles.

## 6. Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)
CPO vehicles receive a 12-month / 12,000-mile comprehensive warranty that begins when the original basic warranty ends, plus the remainder of the powertrain coverage. A 172-point inspection is performed before sale.

## 7. How to make a claim
Bring the vehicle to any authorized Velocity Motors dealer. There is no deductible for warranty repairs. Loaner vehicles are provided for repairs expected to take more than 4 hours.
